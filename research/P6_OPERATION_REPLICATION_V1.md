# P6 Operation Replication V1

## Question

The first frozen pilot was operationally valid but produced identical terminal
outcomes for EnvSolve and its same-backbone free-form ablation. This replication asks
whether that zero difference persists on ten new development identities when no code,
budget, model, evaluator, or treatment boundary changes.

## Anti-Overfitting Boundary

Selection is a salted metadata-only hash from the 133 identities untouched after the
pilot. There is no repository inspection, failure prescreen, package-manager
stratification, or reuse. All selected identities become consumed. Pilot trajectories
may define aggregate measurements, but they do not generate repository-specific rules
or modify Algorithm v17 and Harness v32.

## Contrast and Decision

The paired contrast and primitive limits are identical to the pilot. All ten pairs
run before an algorithm decision. A larger unchanged batch requires at least one
EnvSolve-only Boolean Official pass or reach, no baseline-only pass or unexplained
terminal regression, and at least six eligible Boolean Official pairs. Continued zero
positive discordance or zero guard rejection despite grounded negative-operation facts
triggers aggregate algorithm review instead of more compute.

This remains development evidence. Canary-20 and Official-Test-100 stay untouched,
and no leaderboard or paper-effectiveness claim is permitted.

The machine-readable protocol is
`experiments/validations/p6_operation_replication_v1_preregistration.json`.
