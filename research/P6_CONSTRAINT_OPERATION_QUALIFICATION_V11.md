## Question

With the output and provider acquisition confounds isolated, does the frozen
constraint-driven operation method reach executable and Official terminal states on
new development identities, and does it differ from a matched free-form ablation?

Q11 is a five-pair outcome-blind development qualification. Both methods share the
repository and base-runtime observers, two-layer verifier, model, seed, fresh image,
output contract, acquisition recovery, and all primitive limits. Full alone receives
initial constraint admission, the operation plan, and the typed operation guard.
Official feedback remains terminal and is never returned to the solver.

## Qualification

The schedule runs all five pairs unless a shared boundary invariant fails or the
operator interrupts execution. A paired method comparison requires at least two pairs
where both runs are scientifically eligible and have Boolean Official outcomes. With
fewer terminal pairs, Q11 reports only terminal-reach insufficiency and failure
decomposition; it does not estimate effectiveness.

Every model attempt, retry, candidate proposal, environment, command, token, and wall
clock unit is audited. Provider parse recovery is valid only when failed attempts,
retries, and recovery agree in the ledger. Exhaustion is infrastructure Unknown and
censors the pair without rerun. Output-length and budget terminals must retain their
frozen categories. Reasoning content is never persisted.

No selected identity is replaced. Q11 is development evidence and cannot support a
paper test-set, leaderboard, or final effectiveness claim.
